## DEMO FLOW (5-7 MINUTES)

### **PART 1: DASHBOARD SCREEN (1.5 minutes)**

**What to Say:**
"This is the main dashboard - the first thing users see when they open the app."

**Point Out:**

1. **Risk Indicator (Top Center)**
   - "See this circle showing 23%? This is the overall risk level for this location."
   - "It updates automatically every 15 minutes."
   - "Green means safe, orange means caution, red means danger."
   - "Right now it shows 'No Risk' because we're below 50%."

2. **Location Display**
   - "At the top, you can see the current location."
   - "Tap this to change your location manually."

3. **Disaster Type Cards**
   - "Below, we have 5 disaster types: Floods, Cyclones, Earthquakes, Wildfires, and Heatwaves."
   - "Each shows its own risk percentage."
   - Tap one card: "You can tap any card to see detailed information."

4. **Active Alerts Section**
   - Scroll down: "If there are any active alerts, they appear here."
   - "Color-coded by severity - yellow, orange, or red."

5. **Risk Breakdown**
   - "The risk breakdown shows all five disaster types at a glance."
   - "Quick visual bars so you can see threats instantly."

**Action:**
"Let me show you what happens when we check another feature."

---

### **PART 2: MAP SCREEN (1.5 minutes)**

**Transition:**
Tap the bottom navigation: "Now let's look at the Map view."

**What to Say:**
"This is an interactive map showing disaster zones visually."

**Point Out:**

1. **Your Location Marker**
   - "This pin shows where you are right now."
   - "The color matches your risk level - green in this case."
   - "You can zoom in and out freely."

2. **Map Layers**
   - Point to legend: "This legend shows what each color means."
   - "Each disaster type has its own color."
   - Toggle layers: "You can turn different disaster types on or off."

3. **Risk Circles**
   - "These circles show risk areas around you."
   - "Larger circles mean higher risk zones."

4. **Recenter Button**
   - Point to FAB: "This button brings the map back to your location."
   - Tap it: "Like this - instant recentering."

5. **Refresh Button**
   - "And this refreshes all the data to get the latest information."

**Action:**
"Now let me show you the most important feature - finding emergency shelters."

---

### **PART 3: SHELTERS SCREEN (2 minutes)**

**Transition:**
Tap the emergency help button: "Let's find nearby emergency shelters."

**What to Say:**
"This is where StormShield really shines - finding safe places during emergencies."

**Point Out:**

1. **Shelter List**
   - "The app automatically found 15 shelters near this location."
   - "They're sorted by distance - closest ones first."

2. **Shelter Card Details**
   - Point to first card: "Look at this shelter."
   - "Name and type - this is a Community Hall."
   - "District location - Bangalore Urban."
   - "Distance - 208 km away."
   - "Available facilities shown with icons - water, electricity, food."

3. **Capacity Information**
   - "This green bar shows capacity."
   - "320 spaces available out of 500 total."
   - "Only 20% occupied - plenty of room."
   - Point to another: "This one in orange shows 80% occupied - filling up fast."

4. **Tap for Details**
   - Tap a shelter card: "When you tap a shelter, you get full details."
   
**Inside Shelter Details:**

5. **Distance and Time**
   - "Distance shown clearly - 208 km."
   - "Estimated travel time - about 312 minutes."

6. **Detailed Capacity**
   - "Exact numbers: 320 available, 180 occupied, 500 total capacity."
   - "Visual progress bar shows occupancy."

7. **Facilities**
   - "All available facilities listed with icons."
   - "Water, electricity, medical, food, blankets."

8. **Contact Information**
   - Scroll down: "Manager name and contact number."
   - "Full address with district and area."

9. **Action Buttons**
   - Point to buttons: "Two big action buttons at the bottom."
   - "Get Directions - opens GPS navigation in Google Maps."
   - "Call - directly calls the shelter."

**Action:**
Tap "Get Directions": "One tap and your phone's GPS guides you there."
Go back: "Let me show you the emergency assistant."

---

### **PART 4: AI CHATBOT (1.5 minutes)**

**Transition:**
Go back to home, tap "Emergency Help" FAB: "Now for the 24/7 AI emergency assistant."

**What to Say:**
"This is powered by Google's Gemini AI - it can answer any emergency question."

**Point Out:**

1. **Chat Interface**
   - "Clean, simple chat interface."
   - "Suggested questions at the bottom for quick access."

2. **Ask First Question**
   - Tap a suggestion OR type: "What should I do during a flood?"
   - Wait for response: "See how fast it responds?"
   - Read response: "It gives clear, actionable safety advice."

3. **Follow-Up Question**
   - Type another: "What should I pack in an emergency bag?"
   - Show response: "Detailed, practical information."
   - "Notice how it remembers context - it knows we're talking about emergencies."

4. **Natural Conversation**
   - "You can ask anything in plain language."
   - "What if there's an earthquake?"
   - "Is my area safe?"
   - "How do I protect my family?"

5. **Always Available**
   - "This works 24/7, even at 3 AM during an emergency."
   - "It's like having a disaster expert in your pocket."

**Action:**
Go back: "Let me quickly show the alerts."

---

### **PART 5: ALERTS SCREEN (1 minute)**

**Transition:**
Tap Alerts in bottom navigation: "Finally, the Alerts screen."

**What to Say:**
"This is your central hub for all disaster warnings."

**Point Out:**

1. **Alert Organization**
   - "Alerts organized by severity."
   - "Red warnings at top - these need immediate action."
   - "Orange watches in middle - prepare to act."
   - "Yellow advisories at bottom - stay informed."

2. **Alert Details**
   - Point to an alert: "Each alert shows:"
   - "Disaster type with icon."
   - "Title and description."
   - "Distance from you."
   - "Severity level."
   - "When it was issued."

3. **Tap for More**
   - Tap an alert: "Tap any alert for full details."
   - "Detailed information about the threat."
   - "What to do next."
   - "Affected areas."

**Action:**
"And that's the complete app tour!"

## HANDLING QUESTIONS DURING DEMO

**Q: "How accurate is this data?"**
**A:** "We pull from trusted sources - NASA for fires, USGS for earthquakes, NOAA for weather. Same data governments use."

**Q: "What if someone doesn't have a smartphone?"**
**A:** "Great question. We're working on SMS alerts for feature phones. But smartphones are increasingly common even in rural areas."

**Q: "Does this work offline?"**
**A:** "Currently it needs internet for updates. But we cache the last known data and shelter locations, so you can still access critical info."

**Q: "How much data does it use?"**
**A:** "Very little - about 2-5 MB per day. It works even on 2G networks."

**Q: "Can I choose which alerts I get?"**
**A:** "Yes, you can customize in settings which disaster types to monitor."

## DEMO TIPS & TRICKS

**Before Starting:**
- Clear any notifications
- Close other apps
- Set brightness to max
- Enable "Do Not Disturb" (except for demo)
- Have backup internet (mobile hotspot)

**During Demo:**
- Speak slowly and clearly
- Point with your finger on screen
- Pause to let features register
- Don't rush through screens
- Encourage questions

**If Something Goes Wrong:**
- Stay calm
- Have backup screenshots ready
- Say "Let me show you on the backup screen"
- Never apologize excessively - move on

**Screen Mirroring Tips:**
- Test mirroring 30 minutes before
- Have HDMI cable as backup
- Keep phone plugged in
- Use airplane mode + WiFi to avoid calls

## TECH ISSUES TROUBLESHOOTING

**Problem: App crashes**
- Solution: Have video recording backup
- Say: "Let me show you the recorded demo"

**Problem: No GPS signal**
- Solution: Use manual location entry
- Say: "Let me enter a location manually"

**Problem: No internet**
- Solution: Use prepared screenshots
- Say: "Here's how it looks with live data"

**Problem: Slow loading**
- Solution: Keep talking while it loads
- Say: "While this loads, notice that..."

**Problem: Phone overheats**
- Solution: Have backup phone ready
- Keep phone cool before demo
